01 / The big idea

What is an
invention?

An invention is something new that people create to solve a problem or make life better.

🔍

Curiosity

People notice a problem and ask questions about how to solve it.

🧠

Creativity

They use knowledge and imagination to develop a new idea.

🛠️

Improvement

The invention is tested and improved to become useful.

02 / A simple idea, a huge impact

The wheel

Invented thousands of years ago, the wheel made it easier to move people and heavy objects. It became the foundation of transportation and many machines.

TransportationTradeEngineering

The printing press

It made books faster and cheaper to produce. More people could read, learn, share ideas, and access information.

EducationBooksCommunication

The telephone

The telephone allowed people to speak across long distances. Modern smartphones have expanded this idea with messaging, video calls, and instant access to information.

ConnectionSpeedMobile life

The airplane

Airplanes made long-distance travel much faster. They connect countries, support international trade, and help people explore the world.

TravelTourismGlobal trade

The Internet connects people and information around the world. We can learn, work, communicate, and share ideas in seconds.
